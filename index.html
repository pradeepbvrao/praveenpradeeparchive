THE STORY · 01

Two Brothers.
One shared rhythm.

“Two voices, one vision, countless musical memories.”

Praveen B V and Pradeep B V, popularly known as Praveen – Pradeep, are brothers, musicians, composers, singers, performers and theatre practitioners who have dedicated over 25 years to the world of Kannada music and performing arts.

Rooted in the rich traditions of Carnatic classical music and deeply inspired by the legendary Dr. C. Ashwath, their musical journey spans Sugama Sangeetha, Kannada Bhavageethe, folk, devotional music, theatre, cinema and contemporary musical productions.

With a distinctive creative identity and a passion for musical storytelling, the duo have composed and directed music for Kannada feature films, music albums, television productions, theatre, telefilms and ballets. Their work reflects a deep appreciation for Kannada literature, culture and the emotional richness of its musical traditions.

THE JOURNEY · 02

Across stages.
Across the world.

Over the years, Praveen – Pradeep have performed on prestigious platforms across India and internationally, including the USA, Australia, Singapore, Malaysia and Europe.

Their performances have graced celebrated platforms such as Mysore Dasara, Yuva Dasara, Kannada Sahitya Sammelana, Sugama Sangeetha Sammelana, NAVIKA – USA and Times U Rock. Their television contributions include serving as Jury Members on Zee Kannada's SaReGaMaPa and as Audition Judges on Colors Kannada's Haadu Karnataka. They also curate and present the Doordarshan Chandana musical series Bhaava Navanaveena.

THE WORKS · 03

Every note has
a story.

Their repertoire encompasses Kannada Bhavageethe, light music, folk, devotional compositions, film music and original musical productions.

Notable albums include Bhaava Mandaara, Mallige, Akka Kelavva, Howzat, Bhava Sannidhi and Manasvi, alongside several independent singles and live performance recordings.

Film credits include Aithalakadi, 9 Sullu Kathegalu, Samayada Hinde Savari, Arasayyana Prema Prasanga and Ellara Kaalelyatte Kaala, across music direction, singing, acting and other creative contributions.

THEATRE · 04

The stage is
another home.

Beyond music, the brothers have been actively associated with Kannada theatre, performing and composing music for distinguished theatre groups including Benaka, Nataranga, Kalagangothri, Neenasam and Mico Lalitha Kala Sangha.

Their theatre repertoire includes productions such as Caucasian Chalk Circle, Maavinagudi Colony, The Flowering Tree, Kamsaayana, Maayamruga and Abachoorina Post Office.

MUSIC WITH A PURPOSE · 05

Music that
connects us.

For Praveen – Pradeep, music is not merely a profession; it is a means of connecting people, nurturing creativity and preserving cultural heritage.

They have conducted music workshops, competitions and educational programmes across India and abroad, including initiatives for children with physical and intellectual disabilities and charitable institutions, creating opportunities for participation, expression and performance.

Their commitment to Kannada Sugama Sangeetha and cultural initiatives continues through their association with organisations such as the Karnataka Sugama Sangeetha Parishath and the Department of Kannada and Culture.
